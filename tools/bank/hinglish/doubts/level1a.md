# Doubts: hinglish level1a (418 groups)

- Some regional dish names may be less familiar nationally: Kashmiri (rista, tabak maaz), Pahadi (siddu, kafuli, jhangora), Northeast (axone, masor tenga), Odisha (santula, rasabali), Goa (xacuti, cafreal).
- Spellings vary across India; chosen: pakoda, laddoo, chhaach, gud, rabdi, puri, kaddukas, chamcham, laal, paani. Normalize if other files differ.
- "paneer" appears in "Pakode kis cheez ke" and many dish groups; overlaps between food groups are frequent (aloo, pyaaz, jeera), so the puzzle builder should check overlaps.
- Weakish items: "Momos ke styles" (jhol, kurkure, afghani), "Khane ki texture" (lijlija), "Mausam ke adjectives" (sard, nam).
- Science/Hindi-textbook terms (prakirnan, vashpikaran, sanghanan, apghatak, sarisrup) may feel formal rather than everyday Hinglish.
- "Gaay ki desi nasl" / "Bhains ki nasl" are niche; probably fine for level 1 but borderline.
- "Mausam vibhaag ke alert" words red/orange/yellow/green may collide with colour groups from other writers.
- "Rashtriya pratik jaanwar" mixes national animal/bird/heritage animal/aquatic animal; "gaay" is not official (cultural only).
- Brand-like words removed (rooh afza, maggi, frankie, dettol, crocin, vicks). "Corbett" etc. are park names, not brands.
- Families: kitchen gadgets/utensils filed as "home"; gym and nuskhe as "health"; plants as "nature".
- Gharelu nuskhe (e.g. burns: aloo, shahad) are folk remedies, not medical advice.
- Proper nouns capitalised: rivers, mountain ranges, national parks, scientists, ISRO missions.
