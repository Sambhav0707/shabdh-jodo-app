# level3b doubts (Kannada, level 3, share b)

- Sandhi-changed compounds: some halves join with a sound change, not a plain join: ಕಾಲು+ಚೆಂಡು, ಬಯಲು+ಆಟ→ಬಯಲಾಟ, ಕೋಲಾಟ, ದೇವಾಲಯ (in "___ + ಆಲಯ"), ಮಂಗಳಾರತಿ, ಅಂತಿಮ ಯಾತ್ರೆ. Check that players will accept these.
- Bound-prefix groups ("___ + ವಾದ/ಹಾರ/ಚಾರ/ಯೋಗ/ದೇಶ/ಮಾನ/ಭಾವ/ಗತಿ", "ಉತ್ + ___", "ಉದ್ + ___"): the tiles are fragments such as ವಿ, ಸಂ, ಪ್ರ, ಆ, ಅ, ಉದ್. They are hard but fair only if the UI allows fragment tiles. Single-letter tiles (ಆ, ಅ, ವಿ) may look odd.
- "___ + ಕರ": ಭಯಂಕರ uses the piece "ಭಯಂ". "___ + ಆಗಾರ": "ಕಾರಾ" (ಕಾರಾಗಾರ) is a fragment.
- "___ + ಪ್ಪ / ಯ್ಯ / ಮ್ಮ": the halves are personal-name stems (ಬಸ, ಚನ್ನ, ಸುಬ್ಬ, ಚೌಡ, ಮರಿಯ). Ask whether people names are acceptable.
- Tadbhava pairs: ನೇಹ (ಸ್ನೇಹ) and ಮೊಗ (ಮುಖ) are literary. ಬಸವ←ವೃಷಭ and ಬೇಸಾಯ←ವ್ಯವಸಾಯ are textbook cases but worth a second look.
- Portuguese and Persian-origin lists: ಪಾದ್ರಿ and ಬಟಾಟೆ are accepted as Portuguese. ಹಕ್ಕು (Arabic) and ಬಜಾರ (Persian) are grouped together under "ಪರ್ಷಿಯನ್-ಅರಬ್".
- Sandhi examples: ವೃದ್ಧಿ (ಸದೈವ, ವನೌಷಧಿ) and ಜಶ್ತ್ವ (ಅಜಂತ, ವಾಗೀಶ) are textbook cases. ಆದೇಶ "ಬೆಟ್ಟದಾವರೆ" is the classic example. ಕರ್ಮಧಾರೆಯ "ಮಹಾರಾಜ" is sometimes classed differently.
- Onomatopoeia spellings vary (ಢವಢವ/ಢಗಢಗ, ಘಲ್ಘಲ್, ಕೊಕ್ಕೊಕ್ಕೊ, ಹಿಹಿಹಿ). The "ತಿನ್ನುವ" set (ಚಪಚಪ, ಮುರುಮುರು) is less standard.
- Idiom sets with weaker members: ತಲೆ ಒಡೆ, ಬಾಯಿ ಬಡಿ, ಬೆನ್ನು ಹಾಕು, ಹೃದಯ ಮುರಿ/ತುಂಬು, ಬೆರಳು ಚೀಪು, ನಾಲಿಗೆ ಚಾಚು, ಮೈ ಕೊಡವು.
- Loanword tiles (ಕಪ್, ಬಿಲ್, ಕಾರ್ಡ್, ಸೆಟ್, ಡಾಕ್ಟರ್, ಬೀನ್ಸ್, ಸಾಥ್, ಆಸ್ಕರ್, ನೊಬೆಲ್): these are common in speech. Some may want them removed.
- Rhyme groups ("ತ್ತಿ ..." and "ವು ...") are fair but easy for level 3. ಮತ್ತಿ (tree) is less known.
- Place halves: ಮಚಲೀ (ಮಚಲೀಪಟ್ಟಣ), ಬಳೇ/ಅರಳೇ (Bengaluru ಪೇಟೆ names) and ದಾದಾ (ದಾದಾಗಿರಿ, slang) may be obscure.
- Many groups are mythology or Sanskrit-heavy (ಕಾಂಡ, ಪರ್ವ, ವೇದ, ಷಟ್ಪದಿ, ಅಲಂಕಾರ, ವಿಭಕ್ತಿ). These are school-level but culture-leaning.
- Overlap: pieces such as ದೇಶ, ಯೋಗ and ಮಾನ appear in several prefix/suffix groups on purpose. Avoid pairing these groups in the same puzzle.
